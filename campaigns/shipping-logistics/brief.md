# Campaign Brief — Shipping & Logistics

First vertical in the go-to-market sequence, ahead of payment processing and
marketing.

## Vertical / service being pitched

All In 1 Commerce's shipping & logistics service offering. This is a
core-service offer — **not** the AI implementation offer. Different buyer,
different price point; keep the two sequences and lists fully separate.

## Target segment

- E-commerce / DTC brands and 3PLs handling their own fulfillment
- Signals of fit: multi-carrier shipping, order volume growth, visible
  shipping-cost or fulfillment-time pain (job posts, review complaints,
  public shipping policy pages)

## Positioning

Lead with the specific pain shipping & logistics buyers feel — shipping
cost creep, carrier reliability, fulfillment delays — not a generic
"we help e-commerce businesses" pitch. Relevance over blast.

## Offer

The pitch is the All In 1 Commerce subscription — 7 bundled solutions
(Payment Processing, Shipping & Logistics, Marketing, Website Design &
Creation, Access to Capital, Bookkeeping, Built-in CRM) on one plan,
starting at **$107/mo (Starter)**, with a higher **SaaS Pro** tier for more
volume/features. Monthly or annual billing; monthly plans cancel anytime.

For this vertical, **Shipping & Logistics leads the pitch** — the other 6
solutions are the "and you also get..." close, not the headline. The
concrete hook behind the shipping module is our partnership with
**Kadima Logistics**, an authorized DHL/DHL E-Commerce reseller giving
access to GPO-level negotiated rates — see
[`partner-kadima-logistics.md`](partner-kadima-logistics.md). Still needed
from the account management team before this is send-ready:
- Specific rate savings/benchmarks Kadima can point to for the pitch
- Whether Starter vs. SaaS Pro is the one to lead with for this segment
