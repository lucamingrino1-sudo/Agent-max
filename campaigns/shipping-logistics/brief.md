# Campaign Brief — Shipping & Logistics

First vertical in the go-to-market sequence, ahead of payment processing and
marketing.

## Vertical / service being pitched

All In 1 Commerce's shipping & logistics service offering. This is a
core-service offer — **not** the AI implementation offer. Different buyer,
different price point; keep the two sequences and lists fully separate.

## Target segment

- E-commerce / DTC brands and 3PLs handling their own fulfillment
- Signals of fit: multi-carrier shipping, order volume growth, visible
  shipping-cost or fulfillment-time pain (job posts, review complaints,
  public shipping policy pages)

## Positioning

Lead with the specific pain shipping & logistics buyers feel — shipping
cost creep, carrier reliability, fulfillment delays — not a generic
"we help e-commerce businesses" pitch. Relevance over blast. The CTA is
always the free rate audit (see Offer below), framed as a quick, no-risk
check rather than a sales conversation.

## Offer — Free Shipping Rate Audit

The top-of-funnel CTA for this vertical is a **free rate audit**, not the
subscription itself:

- **Ask**: the prospect's last few months of invoices from their current
  carrier(s) — that's the only input needed.
- **No cost, no obligation.** We review the invoices leveraging our
  Kadima Logistics partnership (see
  [`partner-kadima-logistics.md`](partner-kadima-logistics.md)) and tell
  them honestly:
  - If we can save them money on their rates → here's how much and how.
  - If we can't → we tell them their rates are already good. No hard sell.
- Beyond rates, we also help **streamline the shipping process as a
  whole** — not just a rate comparison.

This is deliberately low-friction: a free audit with an honest "you're
already good" outcome builds trust and removes the risk objection that
kills cold shipping outreach. The subscription (7 bundled solutions,
Starter from $107/mo, higher SaaS Pro tier — see identity docs) is the
natural next step once a prospect is engaged, but it is **not** the
opening ask. Keep the initial send focused on the audit CTA alone; don't
bundle the full subscription pitch into the first touch.
