# Partner — Kadima Logistics

All In 1 Commerce's Shipping & Logistics solution is backed by a partnership
with [Kadima Logistics](https://kadimalogistics.com), a logistics consulting
firm based in Eatontown, NJ.

## What they bring

- Authorized reseller of **DHL and DHL E-Commerce**
- Small parcel, software, and warehousing services for U.S. brands
- Access to **GPO (Group Purchasing Organization) rates and corporate-negotiated
  pricing** — the pitch angle: small/mid-size shippers get large-account rates
  without needing large-account volume

## Why this matters for outreach

This is the concrete hook for the shipping & logistics sequence: prospects
don't need to take our word that this saves money — it's backed by an
authorized DHL reseller relationship with negotiated GPO pricing. Use this
to make the offer specific instead of a generic "we can help with shipping"
claim (relevance over blast).
